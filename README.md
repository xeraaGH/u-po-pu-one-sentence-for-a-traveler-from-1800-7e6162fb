# One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800

A program that writes one sentence of at most 200 characters to `/output/entry.txt`, explaining the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — using only words and things a person from 1800 already knows: a post office and a library made one, holding more letters than any post office and more books than any library, where a word from you brings any of them to the glass in your hand in a breath.
